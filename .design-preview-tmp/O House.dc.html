1O House — housekeeping record, first pass

Eleven screens, phone-scale, in the Broadsheet system. The serif carries all the chrome — no sans, no boxes for structure. Hierarchy comes from the type scale and whitespace, so the app reads as a record being kept rather than a dashboard.

Assumptions I made — tell me where I guessed wrong: staff run 1–4 assignments a day at fixed sites; a submission locks the checklist until an admin approves or rejects; rejection returns the whole assignment with per-row reasons; photos queue for upload and sync opportunistically.

I could not reach the o_house Flutter source from here, so structure comes from your brief. If you can paste the route names or the assignment model, I will align naming and states to the real code.

Checklist rows are 68px tall, checkboxes 30px, primary buttons 56px — sized for gloves. Nothing below 44px is tappable.

All spacing uses logical properties, so 1e is the same markup with dir="rtl". No directional arrows anywhere: rows open on tap, headers close with ×.
