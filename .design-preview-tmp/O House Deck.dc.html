Screen 01

Sign In

Phone number and password only. The language toggle sits above the fields so Arabic-first staff can switch before they read anything else.

No self-service reset

Password recovery goes through a supervisor, which matches how club accounts are issued.

Screen 04

Assignment Detail

A checklist of places with a 68px row each. Photo requirements and rejection reasons sit inline under the place name, so nothing needs to be opened to be understood.

Submit gates on completion

The button stays disabled with a count of what is left, then becomes the only accent on the screen.

Close, not back

The header uses an × rather than a directional arrow, so the same icon works in both reading directions.

Screen 04, mirrored

Arabic Layout

The Arabic pass is the same component with the direction flipped. Checkbox, name, badge and thumbnail all reverse; the type scale and row height do not change.

Logical properties throughout

No left or right values anywhere in the layout, so nothing needs a second stylesheet.

Eastern Arabic numerals

Times, dates and counts render in Arabic-Indic digits when the locale is Arabic.

Place Detail

Instructions, camera, notes and Mark Done in a bottom sheet. The photo control is the largest target on the sheet because it is the step most often skipped.

Context stays behind

The list is dimmed rather than replaced, so closing the sheet returns to the same scroll position.

Photos queue locally

Thumbnails appear immediately; upload happens when signal allows.

Screens 08 and 09

Records and Account

A past assignment reads as a document — what was done, who signed it, who approved it, and what they said. Settings keeps the sync queue visible.

Read-only by design

Nothing in history can be edited, which is what makes the record worth keeping.

Sync is inspectable

The pending-upload count is a row staff can tap, not a hidden background process.

Open Questions

01

The assignment model

Route names and status values from the Flutter source, so the screens use your vocabulary rather than mine.

02

Rejection scope

Does an admin return the whole assignment, or individual places? The design currently assumes the whole assignment with per-row reasons.

03

The admin side

Only the staff app is designed. The review queue and approve or reject flow are not yet drawn.

04

Real photographs

Thumbnails are placeholders. Site photos of the pool, courts and gym would let me check the halftone treatment at real density.
